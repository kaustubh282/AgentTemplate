## What does overseas travel insurance cover

Overseas travel insurance covers specified travel risks during your trip, which
typically include emergency medical expenses, hospitalisation abroad, loss of checked
baggage, trip cancellation where the add-on is selected, and loss of passport. The
exact benefits and limits that apply to you are listed in your policy schedule.

## What is trip cancellation cover

Trip cancellation cover reimburses specified non refundable costs when a trip is
cancelled for a reason listed in the policy wording. Cancellation for reasons not
listed in the wording is not payable.

## Does travel insurance cover adventure sports

Adventure sports are excluded under the base travel policy. Cover for listed adventure
activities is available only where the adventure sports add-on has been selected and
is shown on your policy schedule.

## How do I claim for lost baggage

For lost checked baggage you report the loss to the carrier and obtain a property
irregularity report, then notify the insurer through the published claim channels with
that report and your ticket and boarding pass. Settlement is subject to the limits and
deductions stated in the policy wording.
